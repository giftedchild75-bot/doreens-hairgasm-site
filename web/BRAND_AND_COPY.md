# Doreen's Hairgasm — brand, copy and UI direction

Written 2026-10-26. Everything on the site is grounded in the owner's own words, photos and packaging; anything not confirmed is listed at the end instead of being invented.

## 1. Reference audit

| Source | Accessed? | Patterns worth borrowing (adapted, not copied) |
|---|---|---|
| Luvme Hair | Yes (headless browser) | Calm lifestyle hero with one CTA; navigation in shoppers' own vocabulary — texture, length in inches, lace design, colour |
| UNice | Yes (headless browser) | Trust chips near the top (delivery, returns, payment); help centre reachable everywhere. Its heavy discount pressure was deliberately not copied |
| Wigs.com | Yes | Education first (a wig encyclopaedia), colour transparency, sizing help, clear returns, expert consultation |
| BELLAMI | Yes | Premium through specific, checkable claims; shade-matching help; "text an expert" concierge |
| Mobbin | Signed in, free plan | Beauty/e-commerce screens need Pro; the free plan showed only Sephora's first two screens. Not used as evidence |

**Why these patterns work:** first-time buyers are anxious about lace, length and colour; the leaders reduce that anxiety with education and a human to ask, and they speak in the shopper's vocabulary (inches, lace sizes). A small studio can't match press logos or review counts — but it can match, and beat, them on honesty, real photos and personal help.

## 2. Positioning & voice

- **Positioning:** a personal hair studio that sells online — real pieces, real prices, real help.
- **Headline:** *Your hair, your crown.* (the owner's own tagline, printed on her packaging)
- **Supporting line:** Wigs, frontals and ponytail combos, styled and photographed in our own studio. Every price is on the page — and a real person on WhatsApp will help you choose length, lace and colour before you buy.
- **Voice:** sophisticated but approachable; specific; warm, never pushy. No fake scarcity, no "best in the world", no exclamation stacks.
- **Differentiators:** prices on every option · studio photography · a plain-English wig guide · WhatsApp concierge with context-rich prefilled messages · styling services under one roof.

| Product pages | Price per option, colour swatches, explicit fringe options, her description and specs, *Choosing & care*, delivery/returns from settings, WhatsApp ask-link with the product URL |
